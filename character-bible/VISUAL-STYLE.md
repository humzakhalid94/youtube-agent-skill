# Bobo & Friends — Visual Style Guide

## Current direction
- Cute, polished 3D-cartoon world.
- Warm, friendly, playful atmosphere.
- Expressive faces and readable body language.
- Bright, appealing colours with soft, balanced lighting.
- Clear silhouettes and uncluttered compositions.
- Designed primarily for vertical mobile viewing.

These are the channel's current high-level direction, not a licence to imitate a particular animation studio, film, or artist.

## Composition
- Default aspect ratio: 9:16.
- Keep the main action and character faces comfortably inside the frame.
- Use simple backgrounds that support the story rather than compete with it.
- Use close-ups for emotional reactions and wider shots to establish location or group action.
- Avoid placing important faces or props beneath likely platform UI overlays.

## Continuity
- Reuse approved character reference images where supported by the chosen generation tool.
- Repeat key character attributes in every scene prompt.
- Keep lighting, palette, environment, and prop state consistent across adjacent scenes.
- Do not add text, subtitles, logos, or watermarks inside generated images by default.

## Audio and pacing
- Keep visual beats simple enough to understand without narration.
- Avoid harsh sound effects, sudden loud audio, rapid flashing, or disorienting camera movement.
- Keep music and sound effects supportive and appropriately licensed.
