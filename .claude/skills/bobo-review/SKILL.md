# Bobo Review

Review the episode materials supplied by the owner. Do not rewrite the entire episode unless asked.

## Review areas
1. Story clarity: can a child follow the problem, action, and payoff?
2. Emotional safety: is the conflict gentle and reassuring?
3. Pacing: does each scene add something, and does the runtime fit?
4. Character continuity: names, appearance, personality, scale, and props.
5. Visual readability: clear action, mobile framing, uncluttered composition.
6. Audio: understandable speech, balanced music/effects, appropriate pacing.
7. Accessibility: captions, readable text, meaningful visual cues.
8. Originality and rights: no unlicensed music/assets or copied characters.
9. Upload readiness: owner review of title, description, audience settings, and disclosures.

## Output
Use:
- **Blockers** — must be fixed before production or upload.
- **Recommended improvements** — worthwhile but not essential.
- **Passed checks** — supported by the supplied materials.
- **Unknown / not verifiable** — information or media not provided.

Do not mark an item passed if you have not seen the relevant asset. Do not infer child-safety or legal compliance from a script alone.
