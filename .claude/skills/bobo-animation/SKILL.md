# Bobo Animation

Write animation directions for each approved storyboard scene. This skill does not render or animate media by itself.

## Inputs
Use the approved script, storyboard, character bible, and available scene images when provided.

## Directions per scene
- Start frame / pose
- Primary character movement
- Secondary movement (ears, paws, tail, blinking, breathing, etc.) where appropriate
- Facial expression progression
- Camera movement
- Environmental movement
- Duration
- End pose and transition into the next scene

## Quality rules
- Keep movement simple, readable, and emotionally clear.
- Avoid excessive camera shake, rapid flashing, strobing, disorienting zooms, or overly fast cuts.
- Keep character anatomy, costume, colours, and proportions stable.
- Avoid unnecessary motion that distracts from the story.
- Ensure the final frame can connect naturally to the next shot.
- If using an image-to-video provider, tailor prompts to its documented input limits only when those details are supplied.

## Output
Provide a shot-by-shot motion plan and a continuity review. Clearly distinguish creative directions from provider-specific settings. Do not claim that a video has been rendered.
