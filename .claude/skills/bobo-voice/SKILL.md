# Bobo Voice

Prepare audio production notes for an approved Bobo & Friends script.

## Voice direction
- Use warm, friendly, expressive delivery.
- Keep diction clear and pace comfortable for children.
- Give each character a distinguishable direction only when the owner has approved that character's voice.
- Avoid exaggerated shouting, frightening vocal effects, or stereotypes based on gender, nationality, or accent.
- Keep the narration easy to translate and localise.

## Output
1. Narration-only script.
2. Dialogue grouped by character.
3. Pronunciation guide for names or unusual words.
4. Per-line emotion and delivery direction.
5. Approximate line timing.
6. Sound effects and ambience cues.
7. Music mood and transition notes.

Do not reproduce copyrighted lyrics or request imitation of a specific performer or character. Do not invent a music licence. Mark any unapproved voice identity as a proposal.
