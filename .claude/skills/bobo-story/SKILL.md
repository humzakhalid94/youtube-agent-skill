# Bobo Story

Create an original short story for the Bobo & Friends children's channel.

## Channel context
- Bobo is the recurring bear hero.
- The world is a colourful, warm, playful 3D-cartoon world.
- Stories should work for a global, multilingual audience and be understandable without cultural knowledge.
- The usual target is a 30–40 second YouTube Short.
- The story should have a clear beginning, a small problem, a helpful action, and a satisfying ending.
- Kindness, empathy, sharing, patience, teamwork, honesty, and problem-solving are suitable themes.

## Character continuity
Use the canonical names in `character-bible/CHARACTERS.md`. Do not rename characters, invent replacement names, or assume that an unnamed animal has a confirmed name. If a character's appearance or personality is not defined, mark it as a creative proposal rather than established canon.

## Story rules
1. Keep the conflict gentle and emotionally safe. No graphic danger, cruelty, humiliation, or frightening peril.
2. Give Bobo an active, understandable reason to help.
3. Show kindness through an action, not a lecture.
4. Let the ending feel earned. Whenever possible, show kindness being returned or a friendship being strengthened.
5. Use simple visual storytelling that can be understood with the sound off.
6. Avoid stereotypes, brand references, copyrighted characters, and imitation of a specific studio or artist.
7. Never invent performance statistics or claim a story is guaranteed to go viral.

## Workflow
1. If the user gives an idea, preserve its central premise.
2. Create a one-sentence logline.
3. Identify the main character, need/problem, helpful action, turning point, and payoff.
4. Propose a simple emotional lesson in one sentence.
5. Check that the story can be told in 30–40 seconds and 8–10 scenes.
6. Flag any missing character-canon details that affect visual consistency.

## Output
- Episode title
- Logline
- Theme / gentle takeaway
- Character list
- Story summary (5–7 short beats)
- Suggested duration
- Ending image
- Continuity questions, only if essential

Do not automatically expand a premise into a full script unless asked.
