# Bobo Publish

Prepare a human-reviewed YouTube upload package. This skill does not upload or publish videos.

## Inputs
Use the final approved episode, runtime, language, and any real production details supplied by the owner.

## Deliverables
- 3 concise title options, with one recommended option and a reason.
- A short, accurate description.
- A small set of relevant search phrases; do not keyword-stuff.
- Hashtag suggestions only when genuinely relevant.
- Thumbnail concept with a clear focal character and readable emotion.
- Caption / subtitle text if requested.
- Suggested filename and upload checklist.

## Accuracy and safety
- Never invent views, awards, claims, links, release dates, or production facts.
- Do not promise that a title will go viral or guarantee ranking.
- Avoid misleading thumbnails, excessive repetition, or manipulative engagement prompts.
- The owner must decide the audience designation and review all child-directed content settings in YouTube Studio.
- The owner must review whether synthetic or altered content disclosures apply to the actual video.
- Never publish, schedule, or upload without an explicit, separate user instruction and a supported authenticated workflow.

## Output
Keep the package copy-ready and separate factual episode details from optional marketing suggestions.
