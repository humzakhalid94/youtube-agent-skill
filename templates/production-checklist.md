# Bobo & Friends — Production Checklist

## Pre-production
- [ ] Episode brief completed
- [ ] Story approved
- [ ] Script timed to 30–40 seconds
- [ ] Storyboard has 8–10 scenes
- [ ] Character designs match the character bible
- [ ] Required assets and references identified

## Visual production
- [ ] Scene images reviewed individually
- [ ] Character appearance consistent
- [ ] Props and backgrounds continuous
- [ ] Vertical 9:16 composition checked
- [ ] No unwanted text, watermark, or image artefacts

## Animation and audio
- [ ] Motion is smooth and readable
- [ ] No rapid flashing or disorienting movement
- [ ] Narration is clear and correctly pronounced
- [ ] Music and sound effects are licensed for intended use
- [ ] Audio levels balanced
- [ ] Captions match the final audio

## Final review
- [ ] Full video watched from beginning to end
- [ ] Story and ending are clear
- [ ] Child-appropriate content reviewed
- [ ] Title, description, and thumbnail are accurate
- [ ] Audience designation reviewed by channel owner
- [ ] Synthetic / altered-content disclosure reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Owner explicitly approves upload
